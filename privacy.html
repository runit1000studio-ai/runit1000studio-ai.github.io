Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

RunIt Publisher is an internal tool of RunIt Studio. It is used only by the studio, only with the studio's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts (Run It 1000 / @runit1000.sim and Flag Derby / @flagderby).

What the tool accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's authorisation: permission to upload videos to the studio's channels, and read-only access to those channels' videos and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, engagement, aggregated audience geography).
  • Through the Instagram API, with the account owner's authorisation: publishing Reels to the studio's own Instagram accounts, reading their insights (views, reach, likes, shares, saves, aggregated audience countries) and reading and replying to comments left on the studio's own posts.
  • Comment text and public usernames are used only to reply to viewers and to count which countries viewers request for the next video. They are not sold, shared, or used for advertising, and are deleted from our server after 30 days.

How data is used and stored

Analytics are used only to decide which of our own videos to make next. OAuth tokens and reports are stored on the studio's private server, are never sold or shared with third parties, and analytics data is deleted after 30 days unless refreshed through the API.

Your controls

Authorisation can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Instagram under Settings > Apps and websites. To have any comment data about you deleted, see data deletion.

RunIt Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
